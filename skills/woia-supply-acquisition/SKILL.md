# Supply Acquisition

Read [the acquisition contract](references/acquisition-contract.md) before evaluating authority, readiness, contribution or specialization.

1. Identify actual subject/service/next action with shared identity references and accepted source map revision. Use Core v0.5.3 for Tasks, Due Work, Effects and runtime ownership.
2. Resolve competent action-scoped authority; missing/disputed rights block dependent effects, preserving unrelated permitted preparation.
3. Gather only next-action criteria and accepted evidence versions. UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN. Scores, AI inference, ownership and uploaded evidence do not grant authority. Read approved Knowledge directly.
4. Request genuinely distinct receiver-owned contributions through Core, with correlation/shared refs. Customer Service alone executes person contact and scheduling. Operations/Legal/Finance/Data accept their own facts. Do not autonomously negotiate, dispatch people, post money or mutate another department Task.
5. Use [evaluateIntake](scripts/evaluate-intake.mjs) to evaluate scoped inputs without writing facts or executing effects. Report accepted sources, blockers, permitted next action and owned pending work/review.
6. Require receiver acceptance before transfer; notification is insufficient. Reconcile unknown effects before retry with the same idempotency key. Material source changes invalidate affected readiness, not historical evidence.

Export only the five named methodology slots in the contract. ADD/SPECIALIZE/NARROW cannot broaden authority or change Core mechanics. Do not activate unqualified exact pairs or competing roots. Real Estate delta implementation is W4, outside this base. Local tests are not Operator E2E or Production Ready.
