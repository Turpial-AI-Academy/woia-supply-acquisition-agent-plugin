# Generic supply acquisition contract


## Exported methodology slots

- service-intake: actual supply subject/service/next action; shared identities, no fictional prior stages.
- representation-authorization: source-backed competent decision, exact action scope and Source Authority Map revision; technical access and caller request confer no authority.
- readiness-evaluation: evidence appropriate to next action, version/provenance and competent acceptance. No universal dossier/blanket ready flag. Unknown is not negative fact or acceptance.
- receiver-contribution: distinct outcome only; shared data/Knowledge directly read. Core request/result correlation with receiver ownership; ACK is not acceptance and contribution is not parent completion.
- outcome-continuity: accepted sources, blockers, owner, next review and receiver-accepted transfer. Material changes require scoped reevaluation, not history rewriting or perpetual downstream ownership.

Generic intake/readiness/contribution/continuity stays here. Property/Mandate/Listing/Lease contracts and service routes stay domain providers and the delta. Provider implementation, Core mechanics and organization policies are separate. No vendor/backend/legal rule/private account is selected.

## Composition and recovery

ADD/SPECIALIZE/NARROW only these exported slots. Preserve base-conformance, authority-narrowing, provider-closure and update-recovery gates. One independently usable generic base and at most one qualified vertical form one selected root per context. Exact immutable base/delta/provider closure and evaluation evidence are required; 0.5.x eligibility alone cannot qualify a pair. In-flight Core snapshots remain pinned. Explicit validated rebind preserves evidence/overlays and reconciles unknown external outcomes; rollback does not undo external effects or revive permission.

## Ownership and method evidence

Identity owns Person/Organization, Documents original evidence, Knowledge approved procedures, Data source integrity, competent business/Legal owners substantive rights. Finance owns accepted monetary consequences; no balance invention. Human negotiation/Offers remain human-led. External-person contact/scheduling belongs Customer Service through Communications/Scheduling. Never contact directly from acquisition. Shared data use is not a universal Data proxy; only distinct gaps need collaboration.

The pure evaluateIntake helper consumes references and returns BLOCKED or NEXT_ACTION_ELIGIBLE; it cannot grant authority, write facts, create work or dispatch. It is methodology evaluation, not live provider enforcement. Provider adapters, real host transport, exact composition runtime qualification, Operator E2E and Production Ready remain later gates.
